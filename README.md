# Lakbay-Pinas
Final Project 

Welcome to our platform — a cultural bridge and travel companion in one. Our app is designed to connect tourists with the heart of the Filipino people by going beyond the usual landmarks and attractions. We shine a light on the rich tapestry of Philippine customs, traditions, and everyday life, helping visitors truly understand and respect the local culture. But we’re more than just a cultural guide. Our app also functions as a convenient travel assistant — offering features like tourist guide bookings, curated experiences, and practical tips to make exploring the Philippines smooth and meaningful By promoting cultural awareness and inclusion, we aim to reduce misunderstandings and foster a more respectful environment for locals and visitors alike. Discover the Philippines not just as a tourist — but as a mindful traveler.
