# Lakbay-Pinas
Final Project 

 Our app is designed with a heartfelt mission: to bridge the cultural gap between tourists and locals, especially among Filipinos themselves. We believe that true appreciation of the Philippines goes beyond its beaches and landmarks — it lies in understanding the diverse customs, traditions, and stories that shape our identity. By promoting cultural
awareness and education, we aim to reduce ignorance and foster respect among all who explore our islands. Whether you're a visitor or a local, our platform invites you to experience the Philippines with deeper knowledge, empathy, and pride — helping build a more inclusive and culturally respectful nation.
