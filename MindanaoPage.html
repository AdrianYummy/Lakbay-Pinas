Cities in Mindanao

Davao City

Davao City, the "Crown Jewel of Mindanao," is known for its diverse culture, rich biodiversity, and as a major economic hub.

Explore Davao City

Cagayan de Oro

Cagayan de Oro, the "City of Golden Friendship," is a prime destination for white water rafting and other adventures.

Explore Cagayan de Oro

Zamboanga City

Zamboanga City, the "Asia's Latin City," offers a unique blend of Spanish and Malay cultures, famous for its pink sand beach.

Explore Zamboanga City

General Santos

General Santos City, the "Tuna Capital of the Philippines," is a bustling port city known for its vibrant fishing industry.

Explore General Santos

Butuan City

Butuan City, the "Timber City of the South," is a historical city with rich archaeological finds and natural attractions.

Explore Butuan City

Iligan City

Iligan City, the "City of Majestic Waterfalls," is home to over 20 waterfalls, including the famous Maria Cristina Falls.

Explore Iligan City

Dipolog City

Dipolog City, Zamboanga del Norte, is known for its charming sardine industry and a relaxed, natural environment.

Explore Dipolog City

Koronadal City

Koronadal City, South Cotabato, is the regional center of SOCCSKSARGEN, known for its vibrant T'nalak Festival.

Explore Koronadal City

Pagadian City

Pagadian City, Zamboanga del Sur, is characterized by its rolling hills and a unique tilted tricycles.

Explore Pagadian City

Valencia City

Valencia City, Bukidnon, is an agricultural center and a gateway to the region's vast pineapple plantations.

Explore Valencia City

Cotabato City

Cotabato City, Maguindanao, is a historical and cultural center, home to grand mosques and vibrant markets.

Explore Cotabato City

Marawi City

Marawi City, Lanao del Sur, is the Islamic City of the South, rich in Maranao culture and traditions.

Explore Marawi City

Island Garden City of Samal

The Island Garden City of Samal, Davao del Norte, is a popular resort island known for its white sand beaches and marine life.

Explore Samal Island

Tagum City

Tagum City, Davao del Norte, is an agricultural and commercial hub, famous for its annual Musikahan Festival.

Explore Tagum City

Digos City

Digos City, Davao del Sur, is a growing urban center, serving as a gateway to the majestic Mt. Apo.

Explore Digos City

Mati City

Mati City, Davao Oriental, is famous for its stunning Dahican Beach, a haven for skimboarding and surfing enthusiasts.

Explore Mati City
