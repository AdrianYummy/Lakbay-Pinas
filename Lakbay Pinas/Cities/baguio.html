Baguio: Highland Retreat & Cultural Heritage

5.0 (15,560 reviews)

About this activity

Baguio City, often called the "Summer Capital of the Philippines," is a charming mountain resort known for its cool climate, pine trees, and vibrant culture. Explore its historical sites, scenic parks, and indigenous traditions.

Wander through the beautiful botanical gardens, visit the historic Camp John Hay, or marvel at the intricate artistry of Tam-Awan Village. Experience the lively atmosphere of Session Road and its surrounding markets, offering unique local crafts and delicacies.

Baguio is also a center for arts and education, with various museums, lively festivals, and a thriving local scene that blends traditional Cordilleran heritage with modern influences. This immersive experience will provide you with a deep understanding of the city's unique charm and cultural richness.

Highlights

  • Breathe in the fresh mountain air at Burnham Park
  • Discover the historical significance of Camp John Hay
  • Immerse yourself in indigenous art at Tam-Awan Village
  • Visit the serene Baguio Cathedral and Our Lady of the Atonement Parish
  • Indulge in local flavors and crafts at the Baguio Public Market and Session Road
  • Witness the vibrant local life and stunning mountain landscapes

Important information

What to bring

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Warm clothing as temperatures can be cool
  • Rain jacket or umbrella (especially during rainy season)
  • Camera to capture memories
  • Extra cash for personal purchases and meals

Know before you go

  • The tour involves moderate walking and some uphill climbs, so comfortable footwear is recommended.
  • Dress appropriately for cooler weather and potential rain.
  • The itinerary may be adjusted due to traffic, weather conditions, or local events.
  • Pick-up and drop-off are available from most hotels within Baguio City.
