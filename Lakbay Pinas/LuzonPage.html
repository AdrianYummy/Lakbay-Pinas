Cities in Luzon

Manila

Manila is the bustling capital of the Philippines, known for its rich history, vibrant culture, and diverse urban landscape.

Explore Manila

Quezon City

Quezon City is the most populous city in the Philippines, a major center for commerce, entertainment, and education.

Explore Quezon City

Baguio

Baguio, the "Summer Capital," is known for its cool climate, pine trees, and stunning mountain views, a popular escape from heat.

Explore Baguio

Vigan

Vigan is a UNESCO World Heritage site, a Spanish colonial town with cobblestone streets and ancestral homes.

Explore Vigan

Subic

Subic, in Zambales, offers eco-tourism, adventure parks, and duty-free shopping, known for its historical naval base.

Explore Subic
