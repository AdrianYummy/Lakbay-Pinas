Cities in Mindanao

Davao City

Davao City, the "King City of the South," is known for its durian fruit, Mt. Apo, and diverse cultural heritage.

Explore Davao City

Cagayan de Oro City

Cagayan de Oro, the "City of Golden Friendship," is popular for white water rafting, adventure tourism, and rich culture.

Explore Cagayan de Oro

Zamboanga City

Zamboanga City, the "Asia's Latin City," is famous for its distinct Chabacano language, colorful vintas, and historical sites.

Explore Zamboanga City

General Santos City

General Santos City, the "Tuna Capital of the Philippines," is a major port and center for fishing and trade in Mindanao.

Explore General Santos City

Butuan City

Butuan City, known for its rich archaeological finds, is a historical city and a gateway to the Caraga region.

Explore Butuan City

Iligan City

Iligan City, the "City of Majestic Waterfalls," is home to over 20 waterfalls, including the famous Maria Cristina Falls.

Explore Iligan City

Cotabato City

Cotabato City is a significant cultural and economic hub in Mindanao, known for its historical mosques and vibrant markets.

Explore Cotabato City

Dipolog City

Dipolog City, Zamboanga del Norte, is known for its sardine industry, natural attractions, and the 'City of Orchids'.

Explore Dipolog City

Pagadian City

Pagadian City, Zamboanga del Sur, is unique for its inclined tricycles and stunning views of Pagadian Bay.

Explore Pagadian City

Malaybalay City

Malaybalay City, Bukidnon, is the capital of Bukidnon province, offering a cool climate and scenic natural landscapes.

Explore Malaybalay City

Surigao City

Surigao City is the gateway to Surigao del Norte's natural wonders, including Siargao and Bucas Grande.

Explore Surigao City

Kidapawan City

Kidapawan City, Cotabato, is located at the foot of Mount Apo, offering rich agricultural produce and trekking opportunities.

Explore Kidapawan City

Marawi City

Marawi City, Lanao del Sur, is the Islamic City of the Philippines, rich in Maranao culture and traditions.

Explore Marawi City

Koronadal City

Koronadal City, South Cotabato, is the regional center of SOCCSKSARGEN, known for its vibrant festivals and agricultural products.

Explore Koronadal City

Isabela City

Isabela City, Basilan, is known for its cultural diversity, beautiful mosques, and proximity to stunning islands.

Explore Isabela City

Digos City

Digos City, Davao del Sur, is known for its agricultural products and serves as a gateway to eco-tourism destinations.

Explore Digos City
